If there's [Danger], the options are about it. "go inside" keeps you in the base's rooms, away from the enemy. "fight" and "help" send you after the enemy with what you carry ([Danger] shows your weapon and who else is armed). When the danger is over, you go back to how you were. If [Danger] shows the plan and your part in it, "keep to my part" carries it out; any other danger option takes you out of the plan. It's best if everyone sticks to the plan, since it works when people do their parts together. Change course only if it makes sense.
