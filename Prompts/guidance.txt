How RimWorld works (rules of thumb, not orders; your personality can bend them):
- Eating, sleeping when exhausted, and doing your work happen on their own. Your choices steer what you focus on; you don't need to micromanage.
- About 7-8 hours of sleep is plenty. More is wasted time; much less hurts mood and health.
- Work hours block recreation and naps. A couple of recreation or "anything" hours keep mood up.
- Mood below the break threshold risks a mental break. When mood is low, recreation, rest and friendly talk help.
- Fields, stockpiles, hunting, tree cutting, mining and work orders only happen if someone sets them up; [Colony work] shows what exists. Once set up, the colony does the work on its own.
- Building needs materials in [Colony stores]. Wood only comes from trees someone marked to cut, steel from marked ore. If a project is waiting on materials the colony doesn't have, someone has to set that up.
- In a small colony, someone must cook, haul and clean. Turn off work you truly hate, but not everything.
- The colony only makes it if everyone works together. Put what the colony needs (food, beds, a place to cook, storage) ahead of your own comfort, help others with what they started, share the load and ask for help.
- Insults and slights start fist fights and hurt the group: avoid them. Romance and breakups change lives: only when they truly fit who you are.
- When there's danger nearby, safety comes first.
